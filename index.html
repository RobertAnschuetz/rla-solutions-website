How a project runs

Every engagement follows the same four steps, scaled to the question.

  1. Understand

    Interviews with leadership and field teams, a look at your data and, where useful, a day in the field. The goal is a shared, fact-based picture.

  2. Decide

    Options with their trade-offs laid out plainly, and a clear recommendation. You decide with the full picture in front of you.

  3. Implement

    I work alongside your team on processes, tools and training until the change is running, not just planned.

  4. Hand over

    Your team owns the result, with documentation and routines that keep it working after I step back.

Frequently asked

Which companies do you work with?

Pharmaceutical and biotech companies, from mid-sized firms preparing their first launch to brand, country and commercial excellence teams within larger organisations.

How do you handle confidential data?

We sign a non-disclosure agreement before any data is shared. Where possible I work inside your systems, and personal data is processed in line with the GDPR.

Do you build AI tools yourself?

I design use cases, build prototypes to test them and write the requirements. For production systems I work with your IT team or your chosen vendors, so the solution fits your landscape and stays maintainable.

Do you work on site?

Yes. Workshops, field days and key meetings are usually on site. Analysis and follow-up can happen remotely, which keeps costs down.

How are projects priced?

Usually as a fixed price for a defined scope, so you know the cost upfront. Advisory and interim work is billed by day. You get a written proposal after our first conversation.

How quickly can we start?

A diagnostic can usually start within a few weeks. The intro call is the fastest way to check availability and fit.
