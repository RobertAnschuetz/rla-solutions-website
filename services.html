Two practices, one goal: better commercial decisions

Commercial excellence turns a brand strategy into results in the field. Applied AI makes those decisions faster and better informed. I work on both, and on how they fit together.

Commercial excellence

The structures, processes and metrics that turn a brand strategy into results in the market.

Launch readiness
Go-to-market plans, launch sequencing and readiness reviews before day one.
Segmentation and targeting
Customer segments built on potential and behaviour, not just historic sales.
Field force effectiveness
Sizing, structure, territory design and call planning that reps follow.
Key account management
Account plans and roles aligned with market access and regional payers.
Power BI dashboards
Dashboards built around the KPIs that measure impact, not activity, and around the people who use them, from data model to roll-out.

AI in commercial

Practical, compliant use of AI in sales, marketing and medical engagement, from first idea to daily use.

Opportunity assessment
Where AI creates value in your commercial model, ranked by impact and feasibility.
Next best action
Targeting and channel recommendations that support reps instead of overruling them.
Generative AI for content
Faster content creation with medical, legal and regulatory review built into the process.
Field insights
Turning call notes and customer feedback into signals marketing can act on.
From pilot to production
Data, process, change management and adoption, so pilots don't stall.
Governance
Policies and controls in line with the EU AI Act, data protection and industry codes.
Copilot training
A five-module Microsoft 365 Copilot programme built for pharma teams. See the training offer
