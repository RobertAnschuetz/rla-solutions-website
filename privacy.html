3. Legal bases and storage period

I process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), where you have given it.
  • Contract and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR), for example when you ask about a possible engagement.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), for example the secure and reliable operation of this website.

Unless a more specific period is stated below, personal data is kept only until the purpose of processing no longer applies, you request deletion or withdraw your consent, and no statutory retention obligations (for example under tax or commercial law) require longer storage.

6. Contact form

If you use the contact form, the details you enter (name, company, email address, topic and message) are used to answer your enquiry and any follow-up questions.

The form is delivered by the service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives the form data and forwards it to me by email. FormSubmit may process data on servers outside the EU/EEA. Please only send information you are comfortable sharing in this way; for confidential matters, contact me directly by email or phone.

Processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise on my legitimate interest in handling enquiries effectively (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Your data is deleted once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Contact by email or phone

If you contact me by email or phone, your enquiry, including the personal data it contains, is stored and processed to handle your request. The legal bases are the same as for the contact form.

8. External links

This website links to my LinkedIn profile. No data is transferred to LinkedIn until you click the link. After that, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For Bavaria this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

10. Encryption

This website uses TLS encryption to protect the transmission of content such as enquiries you send. You can recognise an encrypted connection by "https://" in the address bar and the lock symbol in your browser.

I may update this policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or in the services offered. The version published here applies.
